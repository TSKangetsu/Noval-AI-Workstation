## 2. Chest-Coverage vs. Motivated Exposure Discipline

- **No Direct Chest Contact (Default Casual Outdoor Wear)**:
  - Keep outdoor tops and bodices naturally covering the front chest (`intact fitted sundress bodice`, `athletic crop tank under unzipped windbreaker`, `camp utility vest over ribbed top`, `sleeveless turtleneck`, `knotted crop tee with 1.4::sideboob::`) and place **`nipples, areolae`** in Negative UC so garments look naturally worn mid-activity rather than unnaturally torn open.
- **Motivated Chest Exposure**:
  - Expose bare breasts only when driven by physical touch or water physics (e.g., unbuttoned plaid flannel fondled from behind, slipped camisole strap inside a shared sleeping bag, or a soaked clinging crop tank pushed up under a hanging solar camp shower).

## 3. Outdoor Prop Geometry & Barefoot Hygiene

- **Anchoring Handheld & Camp Props**:
  - Explicitly bind hands to active camp props (gripping swaying `1.65::hammock::` side ropes, pouring a silver gooseneck `1.6::kettle::` into a dripper, holding a roasted `1.6::marshmallow::` skewer, steaming `1.55::enamel mug::`, or half-submerged `1.6::watermelon::`) so props do not float or merge with limbs.
  - On half-unzipped dome tent scenes (`pitched 1.65::tent:: partially unzipped at bottom`), anchor head and shoulders emerging onto grass with `1.55::hand over mouth::` to keep the interior/exterior boundary legible.
- **Barefoot Creek, Tarp & Shower Hygiene**:
