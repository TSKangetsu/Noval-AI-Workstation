## 1. Practical Tag Hygiene & Calibration Notes

### A. Preventing Unwanted Human Limbs on Solo Creature Scenes
High-intensity interaction tags (`spitroast, double penetration, vaginal, fellatio, deepthroat`) often pull phantom human hands or male torsos into solo creature scenes. Keep `1girl, solo` (or `1boy, solo` for femboy scenes) and include `human hands, multiple boys, 2boys, extra human arms, extra human legs` in Negative UC.

### B. Belly-Expansion Unlock When Using Slender Presets
If a character preset includes `slim waist` or `-0.40::plump::` in Positive and `plump, curvy` in Negative, omit those waist-flattening modifiers whenever composing distended, cum-inflated, pregnant, or translucent egg-belly scenes (`TN02, TN04, TN09, TN10, TN12, BG03, BG06, BG17`). On internal cutaway scenes, add `fetus, baby, embryo, umbilical cord, skeleton, bones, skull, ribcage, gore` to UC.

### C. Three Creature-Specific UC Nuances in NovelAI V5
1. **Bioluminescent Hive / Fantasy Creatures (`BG01–03, 05–06, 09–11`)**:
   - Block `realistic, photorealistic, 3d, horror, gore, realistic insects, cockroach, fly, dirty, rotting` if you want clean 2D fantasy anime aesthetics rather than horror realism.
2. **Segmented Glowing Jelly-Larvae (`BG04, 07, 08, 12`)**:
   - **Do NOT block `maggots` in UC**: In V5, putting `maggots` in Negative suppresses segmented larva geometry and collapses `worms, small worms, grubs, larvae` into round bubbles. Instead, leave `maggots` unblocked and add `round eggs, spiky, legs on worms` to UC alongside T5 prose (`small plump segmented translucent glowing jelly larvae`).
3. **Multi-Legged Giant Arthropods (`BG13–BG20` Spiders, Beetles, Flies, Centipedes, Isopods)**:
   - **Never put bare `extra legs` in UC**: Bare `extra legs` erases 8-legged spider, 6-legged insect, and multi-legged centipede limbs. Always specify **`extra human arms, extra human legs`** in UC instead, and unblock species names (`cockroach, fly, spider`) when depicting doujin-style arthropod scenes.
