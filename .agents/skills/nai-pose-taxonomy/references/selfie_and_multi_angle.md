## 1. Smartphone Camera Physics (Preventing Duplicate Floating Phones)

NovelAI V4.5 / V5 treats `selfie` differently depending on whether a mirror is present or the smartphone **is** the camera lens itself:

1. **Mode M — Mirror & Glass Reflection Selfies**:
   - **Positive**: `1.5::mirror selfie::, 1.45::holding phone::, smartphone` (+ `1.5::mirror reflection::` for front/rear dual-view shots, `1.55::convex mirror::` for hallway security mirrors, or `1.5::window reflection::` for night glass).
   - **Negative UC**: Add `pov hands, extra hands, 3 hands` so no disembodied foreground arm intrudes in front of the mirror.
2. **Mode D — Direct Camera-Lens Selfies (Floor / Under-Glass / Extended-Arm / Desk Timer)**:
   - **Physical Rule**: Because the smartphone sits outside the frame as the taking lens itself, **block `holding phone, smartphone, cellphone, mobile phone, mirror` in UC** (`DIRECT_POV_UC`) so V5 never draws a second phone in the character's hand.
   - **Angle-Specific Guards**:
     - *Steep floor / under-glass upward POV (`SF03, SF07, SF20`)*: Keep `head at top` in Positive and add `upside-down, inverted, head at bottom, legs at top` to UC.
     - *Chin-clamped downward body look-down (`SF15`)*: Add `face, head` to UC so the camera looks cleanly down past the character's own chest and stomach.
     - *Extended-arm rear peek (`SF27`)*: Describe `one arm reaching back toward camera out of frame, foreshortening` and block `selfie stick, rod, pole` in UC to avoid stray metal poles.
3. **Mode U — Foreground Viewfinder & Livestream UI Overlays**:
   - *Held Viewfinder Frame-in-Frame (`SF13`)*: `1.55::phone screen::, 1.5::camera interface::, 1.5::depth of field::, recording red dot, focus square` (keep `smartphone` unblocked since the foreground phone bezel frames the subject).
   - *Full-Screen Vertical Livestream (`SF19`)*: `1.55::live stream interface::, 1.5::social media frame::, floating pink heart icons on right side, red live indicator dot, ring light reflection in pupils` + block `holding phone, smartphone, mirror` in UC so both hands remain free.

## 2. Motivated Indoor & Night Lighting Ideas for Selfies

When composing windowless indoor, bathroom, or nighttime scenes, omit generic `sunlight, light rays` and anchor the scene's real light source:
- **Harsh Direct Camera Flash (`SF14, SF18, SF30`)**: `1.45::camera flash::, harsh direct flash lighting, dark background, glossy specular highlights`.
- **Warm Fitting Room / Onsen Glow (`SF02, SF26`)**: `warm spotlight` or `warm yellow lighting, steam, rosy wet skin`.
- **Night High-Rise / Neon Stream (`SF16, SF19`)**: `dark unlit room at night, 1.5::city lights at night::, glowing smartphone screen` or `neon-lit bedroom, ring light reflection in pupils`.
- **Steamy Shower / Frosted Glass (`SF04, SF11, SF20`)**: `steamy bathroom, mist, water droplets on glass`.
